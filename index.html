SEC. 01

About

I am a pragmatic, forward-thinking platform engineer. I am known for staying calm during severity-1 outages, and I have built a career around one goal: reducing cognitive load for the developers who depend on the platform.

I started as a junior sysadmin at a logistics company in downtown Indianapolis, fell for Infrastructure as Code, and became the person who could automate days of manual work into seconds. Terraform, Ansible, and Helm were my golden hammers.

Then I built a "Wall of YAML". As the engineering team grew from 20 to 150 developers, almost every infrastructure change became a ticket for my team. A post-mortem for an outage caused by one misplaced bracket in a huge Terraform module made it clear: code is great for execution and terrible for shared mental models.

So I changed the approach. I now champion Infrastructure as Design: visual, collaborative, and guardrailed. Developers compose their environments on a canvas, the platform generates the compliant code underneath, and everyone can see how the services connect.

Today I lead a platform team with a clear mandate: treat the platform as a product, and make the developer experience intuitive, visual, and self-service.

Away from the keyboard, I am in the garage. Woodworking taught me the same lesson from the other direction: measure twice, design the joint before you cut it, and respect the grain you are given.
